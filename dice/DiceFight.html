⚔️ Dice Fight

Captured dice score full size; kept dice score half (rounded up). First to 3 rounds wins.
Playing as guest — your match history won't be saved.
Shadow die (s): can't Power attack. Instead it captures any enemy die showing a value ≥ its own (within the target's range). Skill attacks as normal.
Trip die (t): can make a Trip attack: both dice reroll — you capture if your new roll ≥ theirs. On a miss, both keep their new values. Trip dice don't count for going first.
Poison die (p): attacks normally (Power, Trip-style rerolls — never as a Skill helper), but scores negative: whoever captures it scores −its size, and a kept poison die subtracts half its size at round end.
